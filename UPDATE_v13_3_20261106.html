Evidence and scope

Publication status and access depth are separate fields. Each record identifies the source actually inspected. Bibliography-only or metadata-only checks do not constitute a full manuscript review or a replication. New topic and chapter links are editorial reading mappings, not citations added to the published book.

  • The discovery set is the bulletin published 4 November 2026, covering changes through 1 November. Primary-source metadata was checked on 6 November; this is not a claim of exhaustive worldwide research coverage through that date.
  • Nineteen new records were checked at primary-abstract level. The remaining 17 have primary text, selected manuscript sections or a primary report page checked. Scope is recorded on each entry; no experimental or statistical results were independently replicated.
  • Accepted manuscripts, working papers, research reports and opinion remain distinct from published peer-reviewed articles. The CACM opinion has no new empirical evaluation.
  • Alanazi et al. report internally inconsistent task-time statistics. This unresolved reporting issue is disclosed; the atlas does not repair the values or repeat a significant time-saving conclusion.
  • Historical graph assertions and source-audit fields are retained. Current correction and classification fields identify revised interpretations; new chapter links are reading suggestions rather than additions to the published manuscript.
  • Stable new IDs W194–W229 follow the bulletin order. Original W001–W193 IDs remain unchanged.
  • The full current graph, GraphML, JSON, static catalogues, CSV exports and catalogue ZIP are regenerated from one reviewed payload.
  • W107 and W104 use primary-source v3 records beyond the versions listed in the bulletin. W032 retains both the 2026 online date and the 2027 publisher citation year.
  • The 36 annotations are original atlas text derived from primary sources. The curators receive discovery and classification attribution; their copyrighted November annotations are not reproduced.
  • The existing published-book metadata and all other collections retain their source records. No GitHub commit, push or publication is performed by this package.

Preservation and reproducibility

All previous node IDs retain their positions, and every historical keyed relationship remains unchanged. The published book, other source collections and practice descriptors retain their existing data. The migration uses the explicitly named atlas_polimi_update/1 adapter; routine O/P/F updates keep their original scope.
