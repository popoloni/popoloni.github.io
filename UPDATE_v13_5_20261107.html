How to read a practice link

Editorial classification, not evidence of effectiveness or a literal book citation. A primary classification identifies the most useful editorial entry point for that source; it is not a quality rating.

Evaluates a practice component · 17
The source evaluates the named component, not the entire editorial practice guide.

Review basis and limits

Every editorial mapping carries a specific rationale and an exact excerpt from the source’s existing Atlas summary or limitation. A component-evaluation link names the component evaluated. Historical documentation links preserve the exact earlier-book ← occurrence → practice route. This mapping review does not assert a fresh inspection of every primary text, independent replication, or validation of a complete practice.

Practice pages and source cards provide reciprocal links. The graph offers an overview of the classification and a focused view for each practice. The original Polimi opening view remains available, and all existing node identifiers and keyed relationships are retained.

The published v13.4 baseline had zero direct source-to-practice pairs across all 583 W/O/P/F/B/N records and all 84 practices. Existing topic, family and historical-occurrence paths were indirect.

All source and practice nodes, original IDs, historical keyed relationships and manuscript citation fields are preserved. This release adds relationships and presentation, not new articles or revised practice instructions.

Each mapped source has exactly one primary reading classification. Primary is an editorial navigation priority, not a quality score or evidence-strength claim.

Every non-documentary mapping cites an exact passage of the existing Atlas summary or limitation and explains its specific relevance. Restricted previews and metadata-only source access remain as recorded; the review does not infer subscriber-only material.

Evaluates a practice component is limited to the expressly named evaluated component. It does not claim that a source tested the whole expanded practice guide or used the Atlas practice name.

Historical-origin links project exact B/OCC/PAT routes with their existing file and line locators. They establish recorded genealogy, not independently verified inventorship or effectiveness.

Indexes, announcements, publisher material and non-operational context are explicitly dispositioned. Their exclusion from practice classification is not a judgment of their overall quality or relevance to the Atlas.

Practices without a defensible external W/O/P/F association retain transparent book or chapter coverage; the review does not invent external corroboration merely to fill a cell.

The catalogue exposes reciprocal links, relationship meanings, per-source rationales, source/practice filters and complete coverage. Graph practice views explicitly reveal connected sources rather than opening a zero-edge practice-only preset.

Follow-up review corrected P013 and P029 from reported-application links to explicitly qualified thematic reading analogies. The retained records do not report the specific conversational-code-generation or agent-authorization mechanism of the target practice.
