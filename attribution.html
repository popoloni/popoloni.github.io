Independent editorial additions

Enrico Papalini developed the graph, interface, original atlas summaries, cross-source comparisons, practice genealogy and book mappings, with AI assistance. These are separate from the curators’ original work. No endorsement by Polimi, the original authors or their institutions is implied.

Practitioner collections

The Osmani collection credits Addy Osmani. The Pragmatic Engineer collection retains the named authors, interview and guest-contributor roles recorded in its catalogue, rather than assuming that every piece is solely authored by Gergely Orosz.

The Fowler website collection comprises the 33 essays and memos linked from Exploring Generative AI in the 13 October 2026 snapshot. Individual bylines remain attached to the articles. Birgitta Böckeler is credited as contributor and editor. Hosting on martinfowler.com is not proof of Martin Fowler authorship or endorsement by Thoughtworks.

The earlier books

The 111 B records describe informative files and chapters in five books by Enrico Papalini. The 84 practice descriptors and 87 source occurrences record conceptual genealogy. Repeated appearances of an idea in the series do not create independent empirical support.

Evidence and access boundaries

The website materializes the supplied v12 data. It does not independently re-audit all publications, inspect restricted text, execute source studies or perform a production pilot. Source summaries, titles, dates, mapping terminology and uncertainty are retained. Some earlier-book annotations remain in Italian. W records can contain lead-author-only metadata; use the original publication for complete bibliographic details.

The original 193-source preset, stream memberships, clustering logic and 297 selectable relationship types are unchanged. Catalogue links can open an explicit collection, group or record; opening the atlas without parameters restores the original starting view. Actual manuscript citations and wider editorial placements are distinct views.

Rights and redistribution

The inherited attribution record notes that the SE@PoliMi publication states CC BY-NC-ND 4.0 unless otherwise noted. Third-party works retain their own terms. This package grants no new rights to their full text, subscriber material, figures or datasets. It contains catalogue metadata, existing concise atlas paraphrases and editorial records—not full third-party articles or complete source-book manuscripts.
