Chapter 13: Choosing Practices and Recognizing Their Limits

A guide to the published second edition, with literal citations separated from wider editorial reading suggestions.

Open this group in the atlasChapter catalogue entry

Chapter guide

Selects and combines practices from the observed problem rather than a fixed workflow. It compares proportionate responses, exposes over-engineering and unsafe shortcuts, and connects earlier volumes while asking which mechanisms can be omitted without losing the change obligations.

Scegliere le pratiche e riconoscerne i limiti

Sintesi in italiano

Seleziona e combina le pratiche a partire dal problema osservato. Confronta risposte proporzionate, rende visibili sovraingegnerizzazione e scorciatoie rischiose e collega i volumi precedenti, chiedendo quali meccanismi si possano omettere preservando gli obblighi del cambiamento.

Learning goals

  • select a practice from an observed problem
  • combine practices without conflicting authority or stale evidence
  • justify omitting a mechanism while preserving the task's obligations
Obiettivi in italiano

Obiettivi di apprendimento

  • combinare pratiche senza creare conflitti di autorità o usare evidenze obsolete
