The published second edition

Non-Deterministic Software Engineering, Second Edition, by Enrico Papalini, is the sixth published volume represented in the atlas. On 18 October 2026, the author confirmed that the book was already published. That confirmation is not an asserted first-publication date; no ISBN is inferred.

The final English and Italian source edition 13.7.1 supplies 13 chapter guides and 149 section headings (147 numbered and 2 unnumbered). Each language contains 302 literal chapter citation occurrences. Across the chapters there are 158 distinct cited sources, including 1 resolved auxiliary bibliography record.
