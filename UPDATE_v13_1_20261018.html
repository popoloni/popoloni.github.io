Release 13.1 · 2026-10-18

Practices that can be read and used

All 84 existing practice descriptors now have substantive English operational guides and belong to the PAT Practices collection.

What changed

  • Specific summaries, purposes, use conditions, prerequisites, steps, illustrative examples, verification criteria, outputs and risks.
  • 12 English practice families with symptoms and selection guidance, a family filter in the catalogue and family grouping in the Atlas.
  • Catalogue links open the Practices collection and select the requested node. The inspector shows its complete guidance and related practices.
  • 87 historical occurrences retain their original IDs, aliases, source paths, line locators and recorded hashes.
  • JSON and CSV exports retain the structured content; automated content and browser checks protect the collection during later updates.

Scope and provenance

The operational guidance is an editorial expansion of the retained descriptors and thematic chapters of the author-supplied published NDSE2, source version 13.7.1. Examples are illustrative. Practice names and source genealogy do not establish inventorship or effectiveness.

The published book's literal citations remain distinct from thematic reading suggestions. All existing article and book records, graph node IDs and keyed edges are retained. The opening view remains the 193-work Polimi collection.

The source release is the exact v13.0 ZIP supplied as published. No deployment is performed by the build.

Repeatable updates

The included source/automation/atlas_cli.py supports both reviewed article updates and an explicit atlas_practices_update/1 adapter. The latter freezes the complete English card review and frontend migration, rebuilds from the exact baseline, checks content, graph preservation and real browser behavior, and creates a copy-only delta after verification.
