New topics

Delegated authority and action budgets
Which actions a worker may take, who may authorize them, and which time, attempt or resource limit ends the delegation.
Compatibility, replay and decision history
How a stored decision keeps its original meaning across retries, migrations and policy changes, and when a new decision is appropriate.
Operational diagnosis and recovery
How engineers identify the state an application actually used, distinguish competing causes and restore service while preserving incident evidence.
Concurrency and idempotency
How transaction boundaries, request identity and repeated or concurrent operations affect durable state and observable results.
Expect, Delegate, Observe, Decide (EDOD)
ESA’s work order records the expected behavior, the authority delegated to the agent, the observations of the identified candidate and the engineer’s decision about what to accept or do next. Appendix C collects the four steps on a field card (7 × 10 print source, p. 293).
