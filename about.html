About the author

Enrico Papalini

Enrico Papalini is a software engineering leader and author based in Milan, Italy. With more than 25 years in software engineering and 15 in people management, his work focuses on AI-assisted development, intelligent automation and dependable financial-market systems. His career includes leadership roles at Euronext Securities, London Stock Exchange Group and Borsa Italiana. He writes the Non-Deterministic series and created this independent knowledge atlas.

Adapted from the author biography in Non-Deterministic Loop Engineering. This is an independent author project; the views expressed do not represent Euronext or any other institution.

Why an atlas?

The project connects research records, practitioner publications and the engineering questions explored in the Non-Deterministic book series. It provides two complementary ways to read: the interactive atlas exposes relationships; the catalogues make each record accessible as an ordinary web page.

What the labels mean

W, O, P, F and B identify collections. They are not evidence grades. A paper, interview, conceptual essay, book chapter and small experiment remain different types of source. The graph retains access restrictions, caveats and unfinished verification work.

Biography and photograph

The short biography and existing author photograph were taken from the author’s own Non-Deterministic Loop Engineering materials (About the Author, PDF page 277; printed page 265). The photograph was extracted, resized and saved locally for the website; no generative replacement was made. Current professional titles are not independently re-verified by this website build.
